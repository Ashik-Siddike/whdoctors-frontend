Why Study in Malaysia?

Malaysia has emerged as one of Asia's premier education hubs, attracting thousands of international students every year. With its world-class universities, affordable cost of living, and multicultural environment, Malaysia offers an exceptional study abroad experience.

W H Doctors Study Lab Ltd. has helped hundreds of students successfully enroll in top Malaysian universities including UKM, UPM, UTM, Universiti Malaya, Taylor's University, and INTI International University.

🎓 Top Universities in Malaysia

  • Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM) — Top research-intensive university
  • Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM) — World-class agricultural and science programs
  • Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM) — Premier engineering programs
  • Universiti Malaya (UM) — Malaysia's oldest and highest-ranked university
  • Taylor's University — Best private university for international students
  • INTI International University — Strong industry connections and partnerships

💰 Cost of Studying in Malaysia

One of the biggest advantages of studying in Malaysia is its affordability compared to other popular destinations like the UK or Australia.

  • Bachelor's degree: RM 20,000 – RM 60,000 per year
  • Master's degree: RM 15,000 – RM 40,000 per year
  • PhD: RM 10,000 – RM 30,000 per year
  • Monthly living expenses: RM 1,500 – RM 2,500

🛂 Student Visa Requirements

To study in Malaysia as an international student, you'll need a Student Pass. The general requirements include:

  • Valid passport (at least 18 months validity)
  • Letter of Offer/Acceptance from a Malaysian institution
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • Financial proof (bank statement)
  • English proficiency test results (IELTS/TOEFL)
  • Medical examination clearance
