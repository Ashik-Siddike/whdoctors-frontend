🌍 Life Abroad — Complete Living Guide

Everything you need to know about student life, accommodation, culture, and daily living in your study destination.

Living Abroad as a Student

WH Doctors guides you through every aspect of student life abroad — from finding a home to settling in your new country

🏠

Accommodation

Finding suitable housing is your first priority when moving abroad

  • On-campus university dormitories (cheapest option)
  • Off-campus shared apartments with other students
  • Private studio apartments for independence
  • Homestay programs with local families
  • Student-specific residential complexes
💰

Monthly Budget Planning

Managing your finances effectively is key to a stress-free student life

  • Rent: RM 400–900/month (Malaysia)
  • Food & groceries: RM 400–600/month
  • Transportation: RM 100–200/month
  • Utilities & internet: RM 100–150/month
  • Entertainment & misc: RM 200–400/month
🍜

Food & Cuisine

Explore new cuisines while maintaining healthy eating habits on a budget

  • University canteen meals are affordable
  • Local hawker food and food courts
  • Supermarket cooking saves money
  • Halal food widely available in Malaysia
  • International cuisine restaurants accessible
🚌

Transportation

Getting around your new city efficiently and affordably

  • University shuttle buses (often free)
  • Public buses and LRT/MRT trains
  • Grab/ride-hailing apps for convenience
  • Student discount cards for transport
  • Cycling is popular in some campuses
🌐

Culture & Social Life

Adapting to a new culture and building your international network

  • Join international student clubs
  • Attend cultural festivals and events
  • Language exchange programs
  • Network with peers from 100+ countries
  • Respect local customs and traditions
🏥

Health & Safety

Staying healthy and safe is essential for a successful study experience

  • Student health insurance is mandatory
  • University health clinics available
  • Mental health support services
  • Emergency contact list always ready
  • Know the local emergency numbers

🌟 Top Tips for New International Students

📋

Prepare Documents Early

Keep copies of all important documents — passport, visa, enrolment letter, insurance cards — both physical and digital.

💳

Open a Local Bank Account

Open a local bank account as soon as you arrive to avoid high international transaction fees and simplify payments.

📱

Get a Local SIM Card

A local prepaid SIM card will save you money on calls and provide affordable data for maps and communication.

👥

Connect with Other Students

Join student WhatsApp groups and Facebook pages from your university to get tips and find friends quickly.

🗓️

Organize Your Study Schedule

Balance academics with social activities. Use a planner or digital calendar to manage assignments, exams, and events.

🆘

Know Your Embassy Contacts

Save your home country's embassy contact details. They are your first point of contact in emergencies abroad.
