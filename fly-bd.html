Pre-Departure Checklist

Follow these steps before your flight day

8W

8 Weeks Before: Book Your Flight

Book your flight ticket early for best prices. Bring your offer letter and student visa for proof when booking.

4W

4 Weeks Before: Arrange Accommodation

Confirm your student accommodation. Arrange airport pickup if available from your university or hostel.

2W

2 Weeks Before: Prepare Documents

Organize all documents: passport, visa, offer letter, financial statements, academic certificates, and vaccination card.

1W

1 Week Before: Pack & Carry-On Essentials

Pack smart — check airline baggage limits. Keep all important documents in your carry-on bag, never in checked luggage.

✈️

Departure Day: Arrive Early

Arrive at Dhaka airport 3–4 hours before international flights. Have your boarding pass, passport, and visa ready.

Essential Travel Tips

Advice from students who've already made the journey

Baggage Allowance

Check your airline's baggage policy. Most students get 20–30 kg. Pack university essentials, not heavy household items.

Carry Cash & Card

Take some USD or destination currency for airport expenses. Notify your bank about international travel before leaving.

Health & Insurance

Get travel insurance before departure. Some countries require proof of health insurance with your visa application.

SIM Card on Arrival

Get a local SIM card at the airport when you land. WhatsApp your family immediately to confirm safe arrival.

Keep Documents Safe

Keep digital copies of all documents on Google Drive or email. Physical originals should be in your carry-on bag always.

Know Your Route

Download offline maps for your destination city. Know your university/accommodation address and how to reach it from the airport.
